**Sales & Budget Analysis Dashboard – Power BI**

🚀 **Project Overview :** 

  How are actual sales performing against the budget?
Which products are generating the highest sales?
Which categories contribute the most to overall revenue?
How has sales performance changed year-over-year?
What are the Month-over-Month and Quarter-over-Quarter growth trends?

**📌 Key Dashboard Features**

 KPI & Performance Metrics
Maximum Year Sales
Total Sales
Total Budget
Previous Year Sales
Previous Year Budget
QTD Sales
MTD Sales
Previous QTD & MTD
YoY Growth %
MoM Growth %
QoQ Growth %

**🔹 Interactive Visualizations** 

📈 Sales Trend by Year — Line Chart
📊 Top 5 Product Sales — Column/Bar Chart
🍩 Top Product Contribution — Donut Chart
🥧 Category Sales Distribution — Pie Chart
📊 Sales vs Budget by Category — Clustered Column Chart
📊 Budget Amount by Product — Bar Chart
